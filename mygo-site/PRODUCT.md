# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

MyGO!!!!! fans, BanG Dream! viewers, and visitors who want a concise introduction to the band, the anime, its five characters, and their voice actors.

## Product Purpose

Create a polished Chinese-language fan introduction site that makes the emotional identity of MyGO!!!!! immediately clear, then lets visitors explore the band, anime story, members, cast, and official source material.

## Brand Personality

Restless, sincere, nocturnal.

## Anti-references

- Generic anime database pages with dense tables and no atmosphere
- Unrelated anime artwork or unverified character imagery
- Uncredited Pixiv reuploads, removed watermarks, or misleading official branding
- Template-like card grids and decorative effects that overwhelm the characters

## Design Principles

- Let MyGO!!!!! imagery carry the page
- Make each member feel distinct while preserving the band as one unit
- Use interaction to deepen understanding, not merely decorate
- Keep sources visible and separate official material from fan-provided assets
- Make the entire page usable on mobile and with reduced motion

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA contrast, keyboard-accessible member navigation, descriptive alt text, responsive layouts, visible focus states, and reduced-motion fallbacks.
